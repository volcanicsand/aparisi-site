About the Firm Sobre la Firma

Jaime Winthuysen Aparisi

Founder & Managing Attorney Fundador y Abogado Principal

An immigrant and the son of immigrants, Jaime brings over 26 years of personal understanding and legal expertise to every case. He founded Aparisi & Associates as a multilingual boutique firm dedicated to educating and empowering clients through every step of the immigration process. Inmigrante e hijo de inmigrantes, Jaime aporta más de 26 años de comprensión personal y experiencia legal a cada caso. Fundó Aparisi & Associates como una firma boutique multilingüe dedicada a educar y empoderar a los clientes en cada paso del proceso migratorio.

Based in Silver Spring, Maryland, the firm serves clients across the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area. We understand that every case represents a family's future — and we treat it that way. Con sede en Silver Spring, Maryland, la firma sirve a clientes en toda el área de Washington, D.C. Entendemos que cada caso representa el futuro de una familia — y lo tratamos así.

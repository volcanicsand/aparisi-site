About the Firm Sobre la Firma

Experience. Dedication. Results. Experiencia. Dedicación. Resultados.

Aparisi Law is a multilingual boutique immigration law firm in Silver Spring, Maryland. Founded by Jaime Winthuysen Aparisi — an immigrant and the son of immigrants — the firm has spent over 30 years serving families across the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area.
